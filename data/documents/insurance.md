# Health Insurance Guide

## Claim submission
A typical claim workflow may include patient details, provider information, service dates, procedure or diagnosis information, and supporting documents. Exact requirements vary by insurer and plan.

## Preauthorization
Some services may require preauthorization before treatment. The member should verify requirements with the insurer and provider before receiving a service when applicable.

## Reimbursement
Reimbursement depends on plan benefits, eligibility, covered services, exclusions, deductibles, copayments, and claim adjudication.
