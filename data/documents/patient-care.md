# Patient Care Guide

## Diabetes monitoring
Patients with diabetes commonly monitor blood glucose, medication adherence, diet, activity, and scheduled follow-up. A care plan should be individualized by a qualified clinician.

## Blood pressure
Regular blood-pressure monitoring can help clinicians assess hypertension management. Persistent abnormal readings should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

## Medication safety
Patients should follow the prescribed dose and schedule. Do not stop, start, or change prescription medication solely based on information in this knowledge base.
